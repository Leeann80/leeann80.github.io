🎯 Motion Controllers & Gameplay

One of the most impressive features of this VR mod is the full motion controller support. You won't just be playing F.E.A.R. in 3D — you'll actually be in the game.

Here's what you can expect with supported controllers:

  • Two-handed weapon handling: Hold your weapon with two hands just like in real life. The mod calculates realistic weapon sway and movement.
  • Precise aiming: Look down your sights by physically moving your controller to your eye level. Perfect for those intense firefights.
  • Natural movement: Use your thumbsticks for smooth locomotion. The game world stays stable while you move around.
  • Flashlight control: That iconic flashlight beam now follows your actual hand movements.
  • Interactive items: Press buttons, open doors, and interact with the environment using physical hand motions.

💡 Pro Tip: Start with a slower turning speed until you get used to the VR environment. F.E.A.R. can be intense, and you don't want to get dizzy during those jumpscare moments!

🛠️ Troubleshooting Tips

Even the best software sometimes needs a little help. Here are some common issues and solutions:

🔍 Game launches but no VR output

Make sure your VR headset is fully connected and detected by your PC before launching the game. Also verify that your OpenXR runtime is set correctly in your VR system settings.

🎮 Controllers not working

Ensure your motion controllers are properly synced and connected to your headset. Sometimes re-pairing them can resolve issues. Also check that you're holding them the right way for the game to register their position.

📉 Low performance

F.E.A.R. is an older game, but VR rendering is demanding. Try lowering your graphics settings in the original game's options menu. Less demanding textures and effects can significantly improve smoothness in VR.

🔊 No audio in VR

The mod routes audio through your VR headset. Check your Windows audio settings and make sure your headset is set as the default playback device.
